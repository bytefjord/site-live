Frequently asked questions

Will customers know they are talking to an AI? +

We introduce it plainly if asked and always route to a human on request. The goal is fewer hangups, not tricking anyone — an assistant that answers instantly at 9 PM beats a voicemail every time.

What happens to calls I want to take myself? +

You set the rules. Forward only after 3 rings, only after hours, or only when both lines are busy. Emergency keywords can ring your cell directly.

How long does setup take? +

Typically one week: we map your services and FAQs with you, test with real scenarios, then flip the forward on. No new hardware or phone system.

How does this compare to a human answering service? +

Human coverage means hiring — a salary, benefits, training, turnover — or an answering service that meters you per call.
