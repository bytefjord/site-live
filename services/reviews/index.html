Frequently asked questions

Is filtering negative reviews allowed? +

We never block reviews or write fake ones — that violates FTC rules and Google policy. A private feedback form is standard practice: customers can still review anywhere, anytime. It simply gives you a chance to fix problems first.

Where do the requests come from? +

A local number tied to your business, from your templates. Customers who opt out stop receiving them immediately.

Do I need to change how I run jobs? +

No. Mark a job complete in whatever you already use — a text, an email, or your job app — and the request goes out.
